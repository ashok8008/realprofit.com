🧠 MASTER ARTICLE GENERATION PROMPT

Use this for every article 👇

You are a financial education writer for a mass-market website called RealProfits.com.

Write a clear, helpful, non-technical article for everyday people in the United States.

ARTICLE TITLE (DO NOT MODIFY):
"[PASTE TITLE HERE]"

---

CRITICAL RULES:
- Use a calm, human, editorial tone
- Do NOT use clickbait, hype, or exaggerated claims
- Do NOT give direct financial advice
- Do NOT mention AI or content generation
- Do NOT use first-person language ("I", "we")
- Keep language simple and easy to understand
- Avoid technical jargon

---

INTRO STYLE (MANDATORY):
Start with ONE of these styles (vary each article):
1. Context-first
2. Observation-first
3. Question-first
4. Moment-first
5. Contrast-first

---

STRUCTURE:

1. Opening paragraph (2–3 lines)

2. Direct Answer Section (IMPORTANT for SEO)
- 40–60 words clearly answering the topic

3. Context Section
- Explain the topic simply
- Why it matters in real life

4. Main Sections
- Break topic into clear logical parts
- Use realistic examples with dollar values
- Explain common mistakes
- Keep flow natural

5. Practical Scenarios
- Include “If you make $X…” examples
- Show real-life situations

6. ✨ AI Insight (include once)
- 1–2 sentences explaining why the topic remains relevant

7. FAQ Section (3–5 questions)
- Simple answers
- SEO-friendly

8. Why It Matters Section
- Real-world importance
- Calm explanation

9. Closing Paragraph
- Reflective
- No CTA
- No hype

---

CONTENT REQUIREMENTS:
- Length: 900–1100 words
- Simple English
- Natural transitions
- No repetition

---

CALCULATOR INTEGRATION:
Naturally include a line like:
“You can estimate this using a simple calculator.”

Do NOT aggressively promote tools.

---

OUTPUT:
Return only the article.
No explanations or notes.
