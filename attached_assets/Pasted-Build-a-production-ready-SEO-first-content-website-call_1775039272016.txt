## REQUIRED FORMULAS FOR CALCULATORS

Implement real working formulas, not placeholders.

### 1. Savings Goal Calculator
Inputs:
- savings target
- current savings
- monthly contribution
- annual interest rate
Formula:
- monthly rate = annual rate / 12
- future value projection using monthly contributions
Outputs:
- months to goal
- projected balance over time
- total contributions
- interest earned

### 2. Emergency Fund Calculator
Inputs:
- monthly housing
- food
- transportation
- insurance
- utilities
- other essentials
- target months (3, 6, 9, 12)
Formula:
- essential monthly total × target months
Outputs:
- required emergency fund
- monthly savings needed for chosen deadline

### 3. Monthly Budget Calculator
Inputs:
- net monthly income
- housing
- food
- transportation
- utilities
- debt
- savings
- entertainment
- miscellaneous
Formula:
- total expenses
- leftover cash flow = income - expenses
Outputs:
- total expenses
- remaining balance
- savings ratio
- debt ratio

### 4. Expense Breakdown Tool
Inputs:
- expense categories and values
Outputs:
- category percentages
- pie chart
- top spending category

### 5. Monthly Income Estimator
Inputs:
- average client/project income
- number of projects
- variable monthly range
Outputs:
- low estimate
- average estimate
- high estimate

### 6. Side Hustle Earnings Calculator
Inputs:
- hourly earnings
- hours per week
- weeks per month
Formula:
- monthly earnings = hourly × hours × weeks
- annual earnings = monthly × 12
Outputs:
- monthly income
- annual income

### 7. Hourly Rate Calculator
Inputs:
- annual salary or monthly income
- hours per week
- weeks per year
Formula:
- hourly rate = annual income / total annual hours
Outputs:
- hourly equivalent
- monthly equivalent
- weekly equivalent

### 8. Simple Tax Estimator
Inputs:
- filing status
- gross income
- state tax rate
- deductions estimate
Formula:
- taxable income = gross income - deductions
- apply simplified progressive federal brackets
- state tax = taxable income × state rate
Outputs:
- estimated federal tax
- estimated state tax
- total estimated tax
- effective tax rate

### 9. Set-Aside Calculator
Inputs:
- monthly freelance income
- expense percentage
- estimated tax percentage
Formula:
- net income = income - expense portion
- tax set-aside = net income × tax percentage
Outputs:
- amount to set aside monthly
- spendable amount
- annual set-aside

### 10. Quarterly Tax Calculator
Inputs:
- annual estimated tax
Formula:
- quarterly amount = annual estimated tax / 4
Outputs:
- 4 quarterly payment amounts
- simple due date display

### 11. Self-Employment Tax Calculator
Inputs:
- net self-employment income
Formula:
- SE taxable amount = net income × 0.9235
- SE tax = SE taxable amount × 0.153
Outputs:
- self-employment tax
- deductible half
- net after SE tax

### 12. Save vs Invest Calculator
Inputs:
- monthly amount
- years
- savings APY
- investment annual return
Formula:
- compare future value of savings vs investment growth
Outputs:
- final savings value
- final investment value
- difference over time

### 13. Compound Interest Calculator
Inputs:
- principal
- monthly contribution
- annual interest rate
- years
- compounding frequency
Formula:
- standard compound interest with recurring additions
Outputs:
- ending balance
- principal contributed
- total interest earned

### 14. Investment Growth Calculator
Inputs:
- starting amount
- monthly addition
- annual return
- years
Outputs:
- ending balance
- total contributions
- total growth
- yearly projection chart

### 15. Credit Card Payoff Calculator
Inputs:
- balance
- APR
- monthly payment
Formula:
- monthly rate = APR / 12
- amortization style payoff loop
Outputs:
- months to payoff
- total interest paid
- payoff date estimate

### 16. Loan Interest Calculator
Inputs:
- loan amount
- interest rate
- loan term in years
Formula:
- amortized monthly payment formula
Outputs:
- monthly payment
- total repayment
- total interest

### 17. Debt Snowball Calculator
Inputs:
- multiple debts with balances, APRs, minimums
Formula:
- payoff smallest balance first while rolling payments forward
Outputs:
- payoff order
- months to debt freedom
- estimated total interest
- simple timeline chart

### 18. Rent vs Buy Calculator
Inputs:
- monthly rent
- home price
- down payment
- mortgage rate
- property tax
- insurance
- maintenance estimate
- years staying
Formula:
- compare total renting cost vs ownership cost over chosen period
Outputs:
- estimated cost of renting
- estimated cost of buying
- difference
- break-even style comparison chart

### 19. Cost of Living Comparison Tool
Inputs:
- current city monthly costs
- target city monthly costs
- rent
- groceries
- transport
- utilities
- misc
Outputs:
- current total
- target total
- monthly difference
- annual difference

### 20. Salary Reality Calculator
Inputs:
- gross annual salary
- estimated tax rate
- monthly housing
- monthly debt
- monthly necessities
- monthly savings target
Formula:
- after-tax pay = gross × (1 - tax rate)
- monthly take-home = after-tax / 12
- leftover = take-home - expenses - savings target
Outputs:
- annual after-tax income
- monthly take-home
- leftover amount
- affordability snapshot
