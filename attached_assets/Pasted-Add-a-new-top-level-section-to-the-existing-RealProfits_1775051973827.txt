### 5. Paycheck Calculator
URL: /tools/paycheck-calculator

#### Goal
Estimate net paycheck from gross income in a simple, approachable way.

#### Required features
- pay frequency:
  - weekly
  - biweekly
  - semimonthly
  - monthly
  - annual
- gross pay
- estimated tax rate
- optional deductions
- optional retirement contribution
- optional health insurance deduction
- net pay estimate
- annualized comparison
- bar chart showing gross vs taxes vs deductions vs take-home
- CSV export
- PDF summary export
- localStorage support

#### Output
- gross pay
- taxes
- deductions
- retirement contribution
- take-home pay
- annual take-home equivalent

#### Formula
Keep it simple and educational:
- total deductions = manual deductions + retirement + insurance
- tax amount = gross × tax rate
- take-home = gross - tax - total deductions
- annualization based on selected pay frequency

Do not market this as an official payroll tool.
Clearly label it as an estimate.
